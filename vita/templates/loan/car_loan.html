How Auto Loans Differ from Other Loans

Unlike home loans, which are meant for appreciating assets over 20-30 years, car loans are secured against depreciating assets. This means:

  • Shorter Tenures: Most banks offer auto loans for a maximum of 7 to 8 years.
  • Higher Interest Rates: Car loan rates are generally 1% to 3% higher than home loan rates.
  • Faster Depreciation: Since the car loses value every year, it is highly recommended to keep the loan tenure as short as possible to prevent owing more than the car is worth (negative equity).

Why Should You Use This Calculator?

  • Negotiate Better: Dealerships often try to sell loans based on "affordable monthly payments" by silently extending the tenure. Knowing the math gives you the upper hand.
  • Compare Bank Offers: Use the calculator to see the actual total interest difference between a 8.5% and a 9.2% rate from different banks.
  • Plan Two-Wheeler Loans: This calculator scales perfectly for smaller amounts, making it ideal for superbike or premium commuter bike loans.

Special Benefits for Electric Vehicles (EVs)

The government encourages the adoption of electric vehicles by offering tax incentives. Under Section 80EEB of the Income Tax Act, you can claim a deduction of up to ₹1.5 Lakh on the interest paid on an EV loan. Additionally, many banks offer a "Green Car Loan" with interest rates 0.20% to 0.50% lower than standard petrol/diesel car loans.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I take a loan from the dealer or my bank?
Dealerships offer convenience, but banks where you hold a salary account often provide pre-approved loans with lower interest rates and zero processing fees. Always compare both.
Does zero-depreciation insurance affect my loan?
While insurance doesn't directly change your EMI, banks highly recommend (and sometimes mandate) a comprehensive zero-dep insurance policy for financed cars to protect their asset.
What is a good CIBIL score for an auto loan?
A CIBIL score of 750 and above is considered excellent and will help you secure the lowest possible interest rates from prime lenders.
