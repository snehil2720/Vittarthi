● FAQ

Common questions

What are US stock market timings (in IST)?
Regular US trading runs 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM ET, Monday to Friday. In India that is roughly 7:00 PM to 1:30 AM IST during US daylight time, and 8:00 PM to 2:30 AM IST in US winter. Pre-market and after-hours sessions extend trading on either side.
What is the difference between the Dow, S&P 500 and Nasdaq?
The Dow Jones tracks 30 large companies, the S&P 500 tracks 500 leading US firms across sectors, and the Nasdaq Composite is heavily weighted toward technology. The S&P 500 is the most widely used benchmark for the overall US market.
How often is this page updated?
The date and market-open status refresh automatically. Price data is pulled from a market-data feed and refreshes through the trading day; figures shown outside US hours reflect the last completed session.
