● FAQ

Common questions

What are the Indian stock market timings?
Normal trading on NSE and BSE runs Monday to Friday, 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST, with a pre-open session from 9:00 to 9:15 AM. Markets stay closed on weekends and on declared exchange holidays.
What is the difference between Nifty 50 and Sensex?
The Nifty 50 tracks 50 large companies listed on the NSE, while the Sensex tracks 30 companies on the BSE. Both are benchmark indices used to gauge the overall direction of the Indian market.
How often is this page updated?
The date and market-open status refresh automatically. The price data is pulled from a market-data feed and refreshes through the trading day; figures shown after hours reflect the last completed session.
