When Should You Choose?

Based on your risk tolerance, age, and financial goals

🏛️Choose PPF if...
  • You want 100% guaranteed, risk-free returns
  • You're a conservative investor or near retirement
  • You want completely tax-free maturity (EEE status)
  • You're building a government-backed retirement corpus
  • You have a 15+ year horizon and want stability
📈Choose ELSS if...
  • You can handle market ups and downs for better returns
  • You're young (22–40) with a long investment horizon
  • You want the shortest lock-in for 80C savings (3 yrs)
  • You want to build significantly more wealth than PPF
  • You want flexibility to invest more than ₹1.5L/year

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PPF vs ELSS

Both save identical tax under 80C (up to ₹1.5L/year). ELSS gives higher returns (12–15% historically) but has market risk. PPF gives 7.1% guaranteed. For investors with 15+ year horizon and moderate risk appetite, ELSS typically builds significantly more post-tax wealth. For risk-averse investors, PPF's EEE tax status makes it extremely attractive.

Yes. ELSS invests in equity markets and can fluctuate significantly. In a bad year, ELSS can give negative returns — e.g., in 2020 or 2008. PPF gives fixed 7.1% guaranteed by the Government of India — you cannot lose money. However, over 10–15 year periods, ELSS has historically outperformed PPF by 5–8% annually, which compounds to a massive wealth difference.

Yes! Many financial advisors recommend a split approach. You can divide your ₹1.5L 80C limit — e.g., ₹50,000 in PPF (safety anchor) and ₹1,00,000 in ELSS (growth engine). This gives you the benefit of guaranteed returns + market-beating potential in the same portfolio.

After the mandatory 3-year lock-in, you can redeem your ELSS investment anytime. You don't have to — staying invested longer (7–10 years) typically gives better compounding. When you redeem, gains above ₹1 lakh are taxed at 10% LTCG. Gains below ₹1L per year are completely tax-free.

Yes! PPF has EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) tax status: (1) Your contribution gets 80C deduction — tax-free on the way in, (2) Interest earned every year is completely tax-free, (3) The maturity amount (after 15 years) is 100% tax-free. This makes PPF one of the most tax-efficient investments in India, especially for high earners in the 30% bracket.
