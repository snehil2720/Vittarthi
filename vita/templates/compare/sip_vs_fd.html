When Should You Choose?

Based on your financial goals and risk appetite

📈Choose SIP if...
  • Your goal is 7+ years away (retirement, child education)
  • You can handle short-term market ups and downs
  • You want to beat inflation significantly
  • You want to save tax under ELSS (80C benefit)
  • You're a young earner (22–40) building long-term wealth
🏦Choose FD if...
  • Your goal is short-term (1–3 years)
  • You cannot afford to lose principal (senior citizen, retiree)
  • You need guaranteed, predictable income
  • You're building an emergency fund
  • You have low risk tolerance and value peace of mind
