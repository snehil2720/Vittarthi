Loan Eligibility Calculator – Know Before You Apply

Applying for a loan without knowing your eligibility can lead to application rejections, which negatively impacts your CIBIL score. Our Loan Eligibility Calculator uses standard banking algorithms to tell you exactly how much Home Loan, Personal Loan, or Car Loan you qualify for based on your income and credit profile.

What is FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio)?

Banks do not allow you to spend your entire salary on loan EMIs. They use a metric called FOIR. Generally, banks assume that you need at least 40% to 50% of your income for living expenses.

  • If your salary is below ₹50,000, banks usually cap total EMIs at 50% of your income.
  • If your salary is higher, banks may allow up to 60% of your income to go towards EMIs.
  • The calculator automatically deducts your existing EMIs from this limit to find your "New EMI Capacity".

How Does Credit Score Affect Eligibility?

Your CIBIL score is a reflection of your past repayment behavior. It directly impacts both loan approval and the loan amount:

  • 750 - 900 (Excellent): High chances of approval, maximum loan amount, and lowest interest rates.
  • 650 - 749 (Fair/Good): Moderate chances of approval. The bank might reduce the loan amount or charge a higher interest rate to cover the risk.
  • Below 650 (Poor): High chance of rejection. It is advisable to improve your score by clearing outstanding credit card bills before applying for a new loan.

Tips to Increase Your Loan Eligibility

If the calculator shows an eligible amount lower than what you need for your dream home or car, try these strategies:

  • Opt for a Longer Tenure: Increasing the loan duration from 15 years to 20 years reduces the monthly EMI, allowing the bank to sanction a larger principal amount.
  • Add a Co-Applicant: Adding an earning spouse or parent as a co-applicant allows the bank to combine both incomes, significantly boosting the FOIR limit.
  • Prepay Existing Debt: Foreclosing a small existing personal loan or clearing credit card EMIs frees up your monthly capacity for the new, larger loan.
